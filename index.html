About Infinite Building Solutions

Started in 2018 by people with more than 18 years in construction, architecture and interiors, in India and abroad.

We set up Infinite to build designer-specific buildings: homes and institutions where the architect’s drawing matters and the finish shows. We handle civil and architectural construction, interiors, and the technical packages in between.

Our mission is simple: adapt, transform, innovate and repeat. We aim to be trustworthy, professional and ethical, and to build a relationship with each client that outlasts the project.

How we’re set up

A lean structure keeps decisions close to the site. Site engineers report to the founder, supervisors report to engineers, and a quantity surveyor, accounts and HR support every project.

Our in-house crew covers masons, carpenters, painters and helpers, so work is not handed off between contractors.
