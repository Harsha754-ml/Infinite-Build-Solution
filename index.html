About us

With 18+ years of vast experience in construction, architecture, latest technology and interiors, with Indian and international working knowledge, thorough practices and an understanding of industrial requirements in construction, we aim at new challenges, with high capability to solve maximum construction problems and targeting new altitudes. We are now coming to you with a brand name of “Infinite Building Solutions”.
