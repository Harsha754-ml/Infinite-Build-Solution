Meet Our Founder & CEO

Sharath Kumar Gowdara Basavalingappa is the Founder and CEO of Infinite Building Solutions, bringing over 18+ years of extensive, multidimensional expertise in civil engineering, architectural design, and turnkey project execution across India and the Middle East.

Under his leadership, Infinite Building Solutions was established in 2018 to bridge the critical gap between complex architectural visions and precision on-site construction delivery. Having overseen major landmark developments including high-capacity university auditoriums, heavy civil infrastructure, and overseas residential complexes in Muscat, Sultanate of Oman, he personally guides every project with uncompromising technical rigor.

“We don’t just construct buildings; we build lasting trust through engineering excellence, complete transparency, and a relentless commitment to exceeding our clients’ expectations.”
